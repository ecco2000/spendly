Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to save your expenses, and to show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.
